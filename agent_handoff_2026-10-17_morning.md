## 1. Definitions used throughout

- **Model / workload.** 10x4 square Heisenberg cylinder (40 spins), adaptive CBE-DMRG ground state,
  S^z quench at the centre site. The observable is G(x,t) = ⟨Ω|S^z_x(t) S^z_c|Ω⟩, on the scale
  G(c,0) = 1/4.
- **Arms.**
  - `tdvp2e`: `tdvp2_step!` with Saad-stopped exponentials at `tol_expv`, discarding by the BUG's
    own rule (`weight_tol` = `tol_trunc`, via `_budget_svd`). **Since s26 (USER 2026-10-17) the
    rule is the same CUT FOR CUT:** every two-site update gets the allowance
    `tol_trunc·‖ψ‖/_cut_divisor(L, root)` that `truncate_recursive!` gives each bond of the BUG's
    S-step result. Before s26 it was a per-step budget, `tol/(2√(L−1))` per cut, about 1.4x tighter
    than the BUG's. All earlier tdvp2e runs (≤ s25) used the old rule.
  - `bug2`: `cbe_bug_step!(...; order = 2)`, the Krylov-closure BUG.
  - `cbe1se`: 1-site CBE-TDVP, Saad-stopped, but with a *relative* singular-value cutoff (it has no
    budget rule).
- **Fair settings.** `tol_trunc` = `tol_expv` = 1e-6 for every arm; `tol_basis` is BUG-only (the
  RSVD/closure tolerance). Same dt, 4 CPUs, JIT counted.
- **Error.** `matched_error.py`: max_x |G − G_ref|. The reference is the mean of two independent
  high-cap runs; its uncertainty is their half-difference. An error is "resolved" only above 3x that
  uncertainty.

## 3. Findings for the current priority

### 3.1 Errors under fair testing — what is known

- **At `tol_basis` 1e-3, bug2 and tdvp2e agree.**
  - D = 1024: the two references (bug2 1e-3 vs tdvp2e) differ by at most 7.9e-6 in G up to t = 1.7
    (half-difference 3.3e-6 at t = 0.5, 5.5e-6 at t = 1).
  - D = 512: infidelity between the two arms at t = 0.3 is 7.4e-8.
  - This is the strongest "comparable errors" evidence so far, but only to t = 1.7.
- **Loose `tol_basis` is not comparable.** At 1e-2 or looser, bug2's error at t = 1 is 30–130x
  tdvp2e's (task 4). In this window D = 512 barely binds, so tdvp2e is close to exact. bug2's
  basis error grows like ≈ 0.018·`tol_basis` per unit time.
- **Small clusters vs exact.** Above the crossover cap the BUG matched TDVP2 on 4x4 square, 4x4
  J1-J2 and 18-site kagome (`TASK4_VALIDATION_RESULTS.md`). At a binding cap with loose
  `tol_trunc` (1e-4) the BUG is much worse (24-site kagome: 6e-3 vs 9e-5).
  - Every one of these used the old tolerances 1e-5/1e-4/1e-4 and unequal discard rules, so **none
    is a fair-rule result yet.**
- **Open.** No fair-rule comparison covers the full window (t up to 8–10), where tdvp2e's own
  truncation error grows by 2–3 orders of magnitude (discarded per step: 1e-5 → 3e-3 at D = 1024,
  Fig. 7b). `t10knob` and the Phase 6 runs answer this (§5).

### 3.2 Bond-dimension growth — what is known

- **There is no clean measurement yet.** Every fair-rule run so far used a binding cap (χ = 512 or
  1024 for both arms from step 1), which says nothing about growth.
- **Discarded norm at the same binding cap** (kscreen2, D = 512, same rule): bug2 discards *less*
  per step than tdvp2e (bug2 1e-2 + fold: 1.4e-5 → 2.4e-4 over steps 1–10; tdvp2e: 7.7e-5 →
  3.1e-4). This is suggestive only: bug2's step solves in a 2χ trial space, then compresses once.
- **χ in the Phase 6 test at D = 1024:** tdvp2e 1024, bug2 1024 (both cap-bound), cbe1se 780
  (relative cutoff 1e-6, not the budget rule).
- **Working rank vs final rank.** bug2's *working* frames (closure) grow to 1.4–2χ even though the
  *final* χ is capped (Fig. 5b, ref1024b at 2048). That is memory and cost, not the final bond
  dimension, but report it alongside χ.
- **Prior evidence goes against the BUG** (memory `tdvp2-carries-the-smallest-rank-not-bug`). In
  the scar model (L = 20 LR-TFIM, non-binding cap, legacy BUG, TDVP with a relative cutoff), the
  rank order was bug > bugmid > tdvp2 at every time, with bug 17–36 % above tdvp2 and identical
  entropies. That was under unequal discard rules; it must be re-measured under the budget rule.
- **χ is set by where you cut.** Always report χ(t) together with the tolerance and the matched
  error.

### 3.3 Cost findings (for the later efficiency stage)

- **Where bug2's time goes.** The half-sweeps cost ~45 s of a D = 512 step, under half of tdvp2e's
  ~100 s. The order-2 closure costs 45–64 s and grows with t; at D = 1024 it grows faster.
- **Cost structure.** The closure needs about 3 extra sweeps whenever j = 1 fails its share. Its
  share is `tol_basis/sqrt((L−1)·order)`, tied to `tol_basis` alone (`basis_allocation`,
  `adaptive_budget.jl`; closure in `krylov_closure.jl`).
- **Approval needed.** Decoupling the closure share at a binding cap (handover-4 task 3) changes the
  plan's tolerance derivation, so it needs user approval.
- **Early-time edge at D = 1024.** bug2 is faster at early times (1.56x on one node, task 9); it
  loses this as its frames grow.

### 4d. Same discarded singular values per cut, not per step (USER 2026-10-17)

> "we should not discard the norm per step. we need to make sure the singular values that are
> being discarded for the time evolution step is the same, so the S-step for the BUG and the per
> bond time update for the 2sitetdvp. we can make the augmentation steps as loose and efficient as
> possible while maintaining similar error"

- **Before:** both arms bounded the step's total relative discarded norm by `tol_trunc`, but
  shared it differently:
  - BUG: L−1 cuts, each `tol/(√n_L+√n_R)`, which is `tol/6.78` at L = 24;
  - tdvp2e: 2(L−1) cuts, each `tol/(2√(L−1))`, which is `tol/9.59`.
- **Now:** one helper, `_cut_divisor(L, root)` (`zero_site_core.jl`), is used by both
  `truncate_recursive!` and `tdvp2_step!(...; weight_tol)`, with root = `_bug_root_link(L) + 1`,
  the BUG's default (`cbe_bug_step!` now calls `_bug_root_link` too).
  - tdvp2's `info.cumulative` is now reported, not bounded: at most √(2(L−1))/(√n_L+√n_R) ≈ √2
    times `tol_trunc`.
  - The BUG is unchanged; plan §5's `tol_trunc` still bounds its S-step compression.
- **Test** (`test_tdvp2_truncation.jl`): both arms' worst cut fits `tol/_cut_divisor`; tdvp2's
  total fits 2√(L−1) times that; a non-vacuity check shows tdvp2 cuts past the old, tighter share.
